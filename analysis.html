Nitrate occurrences

Number and percentage of samples in each nitrate result band. The left-hand scale shows the number of samples; colours follow the FreshWater Watch nitrate scale.

Phosphate occurrences

Number and percentage of samples in each phosphate result band. The left-hand scale shows the number of samples; colours follow the FreshWater Watch phosphate scale.

Turbidity occurrences

Number and percentage of samples across the full FreshWater Watch turbidity scale. The left-hand scale shows the number of samples; colours follow the existing turbidity assessment scale.

Temperature

Recorded water temperature results over time.

pH

Recorded pH results over time. Result colours follow the supplied pH 4–10 test-kit colour card, with each reading identified as acidic, neutral or alkaline; the water-quality assessment is shown separately.

Coliform

Total coliform colony results per 100 mL. Test-kit colour indicates a positive or negative result; the separate detection indicator is not an official bathing-water classification.

Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD)

Recorded BOD results over time.

Dissolved Oxygen

Recorded dissolved oxygen results over time. Result colours follow the supplied 0, 4 and 8 ppm test-kit colour card; percent saturation is calculated from the supplied temperature/oxygen table.

Each point represents the recorded result for that survey date at the selected site. Nitrate and phosphate are plotted using the midpoint of the FreshWater Watch result range. Turbidity is plotted in NTU using the recorded test value.
