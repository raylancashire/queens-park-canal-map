How to interpret the results

Low Moderate High

The indicative nutrient categories follow the FreshWater Watch chart provided for Canal Watch. Higher test bands indicate higher nutrient concentrations; they do not establish overall ecological status on their own.

Results collected on different dates are not simultaneous comparisons. Where a result falls in the same test band as a previous survey, a change within that band cannot be determined.

Open-ended bands (such as >10) have no defined upper limit. Their displayed position represents the highest test band, not an assumed numerical concentration.
